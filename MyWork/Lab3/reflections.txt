I broke down the problem into reversing a standard LL calcualting how many K sized chunks fit into the total size
with an arrat which is a fixed block of memory stored in the heap. To reverse a chunk of size 'k', by just using two index variables
a left and a right pointer marking the start and end of a chunk and physically swap the values moving inward until the pointers meet.

The linked list approach was honestly a lot trickier to conceptualize because we aren't moving
the data at all we are completely rewiring the architecture of the list.
Instead of swapping values, we have to manipulate the next
references while changing and keep track of the temporary pointers (prev, curr, nextNode) just to keep the list intact.
